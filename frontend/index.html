About Me

Hi, I’m Ethan Passino — a passionate and driven software engineer with a deep love for building practical, impactful applications.

I have over a year of professional software engineering experience, building and improving full-stack applications with React, TypeScript, Node.js, and modern testing tools. My work includes user-facing workflows, data migrations, backend services, and features designed to make complex processes easier to use.

I earned my Computer Science degree from Purdue University Fort Wayne, where I developed a strong foundation in data structures, algorithms, system design, and software development.

I enjoy turning ambiguous problems into reliable software—whether that means creating a polished interface, designing a maintainable backend, improving data quality, or expanding automated test coverage.
