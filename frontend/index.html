About me

I’m Ethan, a software engineer and computer science graduate from Purdue University Fort Wayne. I build web apps, including editors that let people set up their own workflows.

When I’m building a feature, I like talking through how people will use it and where they might get stuck. Those conversations help me figure out what to build and what to test.
